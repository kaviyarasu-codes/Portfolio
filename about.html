I started with Python, Django and MySQL, built and launched client websites during my internship at Aero Edge Media, and now work as the App Developer at The Shine, a printing, events and rentals company in Coimbatore.

There I handle the company's digital products end to end. I built the Agnisiragu news platform entirely on my own — two Android apps (the reader app is live on Google Play), the NestJS backend, the admin panel and the website. I'm now building the TicketsRaja ticketing marketplace, an in-house GST billing platform and the Event247 website, and I run the servers, domains and Play Store releases behind them.

How I work: understand what the business actually needs, plan it properly, build it clean, ship it — then keep it running. AI-assisted tools help me move faster without cutting corners.
