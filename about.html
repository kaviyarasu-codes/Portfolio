Building the Web — and Apps — One Project at a Time

I'm a full-stack and app developer with a B.E. in Computer Science. I started with Python, Django and MySQL, built and launched client websites during my internship at Aero Edge Media, and now work as the App Developer at The Shine, a printing, events and rentals company in Coimbatore.

At The Shine I handle the company's digital products end to end: the Agnisiragu Tamil news app (live on Google Play) and its Next.js website, the TicketsRaja ticketing marketplace, an in-house GST billing platform and the Event247 website. I also run the servers, domains and Play Console releases behind them.

My approach is simple: understand what the business actually needs, plan it properly, build it clean, and ship it — then keep it running. I use AI-assisted tools to move faster without cutting corners.
