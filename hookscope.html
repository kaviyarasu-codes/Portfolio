HookScope

An ad pre-testing studio. Upload two or three versions of an ad and see which one holds attention, and where viewers will drop, before you spend on it.

Ask for a demo How it works

The problem

A marketing team finds out an ad was weak only after the money is spent: low click-through, high cost per lead. HookScope gives a second opinion before that.

Which version?

Two or three cuts of the same ad, compared on one scale, with a clear pick or an honest "too close to call".

Where do people leave?

The second where interest falls sharply, slides slowly or stays low, so the editor knows exactly what to cut.

What to fix?

A short to-do list in plain words: open with your strongest moment, simplify the words, add a pattern break.

How it works

A research model from Meta's AI lab does the hard part. HookScope turns its output into decisions.

  1. 01

    Upload

    1 to 3 versions: video, audio or a script. English, or Tamil with the words translated for the model.

  2. 02

    Predict

    Meta FAIR's TRIBE v2 runs on a cloud GPU and predicts an average viewer's brain response, second by second.

  3. 03

    Score

    Overall score, hook (first 3 s), strongest 10 s, drop-offs, slow declines, weak stretches and four brain systems.

  4. 04

    Decide and learn

    "What to do" first. Real results go back in, and the studio learns what works for this audience.

Inside the studio

Ask in Claude, in Tamil or English

HookScope is also a Claude connector. The team adds it once in Claude, signs in with their studio account, and then simply asks:

"Test these two Drive links and tell me which one to run."

Claude starts the test, reads the report when it is ready and answers with the decision first, then the evidence. It can also list past tests, explain what the studio has learned and save real results.

  • MCP connector with six tools: test, report, retry, list, insights, save results
  • OAuth 2.1 sign-in with the studio's own accounts; no shared passwords
  • Answers in the person's language, Tamil or English

  • Turned the model's 20,484 brain-surface points into four plain systems (visual, sound, words, attention) with a brain atlas, then into 0 to 100 scores.
  • Wrote detectors for sharp drops, slow declines and weak stretches after the first real ad showed a slow 15-second slide that a simple drop check missed.
  • Tamil support: speech is transcribed and translated by Whisper, then the English words are timed back onto the video for the model.
  • A learning layer: once 12 or more past ads have real results, a small model predicts CTR or cost per lead, and shows it only if it was accurate on ads it had not seen.
  • Runs on the client's own server with their own logins; videos are deleted after a set number of days, reports are kept, and backups rotate automatically.

Honest notes

Predictions, not measurements

The model predicts an average viewer's response. It is a second opinion next to the team's judgement and real results.
