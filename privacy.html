Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This website is the personal portfolio of Kaviyarasu K, a developer in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. This page explains what happens to the details you share with me. I keep it short and plain.

What I collect

  • Contact form: your first name, last name (optional), email address, subject and message.
  • Footer email box: the email address you type, which only pre-fills the contact form.
  • Technical data: my hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard request data such as IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the site. I do not use analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies.
  • Your theme choice: light or dark mode is saved in your own browser (localStorage). It never leaves your device.

Why I use it

Only to read and reply to your message, for example about a project, a job or a question. I do not sell, rent or share your details for marketing, and I do not add you to any mailing list.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore): stores contact form messages. Only I can read them, after signing in.
  • EmailJS: sends me a notification email and sends you an automatic reply.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts: provides the font, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download it.

These services may process data outside India under their own privacy and security terms.

How long I keep it

I keep messages for up to 24 months after our last conversation, then delete them, unless we work together and I need them for that work or for legal reasons.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (and similar laws where you live), you can ask me to show, correct or delete the details you sent, or withdraw your consent. Email kaviyarasukanthavel@gmail.com and I will reply within 30 days.

Security

The site uses HTTPS, strict browser security headers, and database rules that let visitors only add a message, never read one. No method is perfect, so please do not send passwords, card numbers or other sensitive information through the form.

Children

This site is meant for adults and businesses. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before sending your details.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
