Audit a website, web app or Android app

Checks only what a normal visitor's browser sees: every page, its links and files, the public JavaScript, security headers, cookies, robots/sitemap and public DNS. Nothing is submitted, guessed or attacked.

    Site Inspector browser extension

    See what any website is built with (CMS, frameworks and versions, hosting, analytics), plus a Design tab that finds low-contrast text (with a fixed colour), hidden or covered buttons, tiny tap targets, cut-off text, broken or blurry images and more, with "Show" to highlight each one on the page. It runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. "Full audit" opens this page with the address filled in.

    1. Download the zip below and unzip it to a folder you will keep.
    2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
    3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
    4. Pin the extension, open any website and click its icon. Right-click a page for "Full Site Audit".
